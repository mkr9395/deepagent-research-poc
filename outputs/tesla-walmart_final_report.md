# Tesla vs. Walmart: FY2025 revenue and profitability

## Executive Summary
- Walmart generated **$681.0 billion in revenue**, about **7.18 times** Tesla’s $94.8 billion; its revenue grew **5.07%**, while Tesla’s fell **2.93%**. [1–4]
- Walmart’s attributable net income was **$19.44 billion**, versus Tesla’s **$3.79 billion**. Tesla nonetheless had the higher calculated net margin: **4.00% versus 2.85%**. [1–4]
- **The periods are not aligned:** Walmart’s FY2025 ended January 31, 2025; Tesla’s ended December 31, 2025. They overlap for only one month. [1,3]

## Revenue and profitability comparison

Dollar figures are US$ billions. Margins and year-over-year growth are calculated from the reported figures.

| Measure | Tesla FY2025 | Walmart FY2025 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Revenue | $94.827 [1,2] | $680.985 [3] |
| Revenue growth vs. FY2024 | −2.93% [2] | +5.07% [3] |
| Net income attributable to owners | $3.794 [1,2] | $19.436 [4] |
| Net margin | 4.00% [2] | 2.85% [3,4] |
| Operating income | $4.355 [2] | $29.348 [3] |
| Operating margin | 4.59% [2] | 4.31% [3] |

**Key differences — revenue:** Walmart’s revenue was approximately **$586.16 billion greater**; its growth rate exceeded Tesla’s by **8.00 percentage points** (calculated). These comparisons use different annual periods. [1–3]

**Key differences — profitability:** Walmart earned approximately **$15.64 billion more** in attributable net income, while Tesla’s net margin was higher by **1.15 percentage points** and its operating margin by **0.28 percentage points** (calculated). This suggests Tesla retained more income per revenue dollar in its fiscal year, whereas Walmart earned more in absolute dollars. [1–4]

## Overall Comparison
Walmart was substantially larger by both revenue and net income and reported positive revenue growth; Tesla had modestly higher margins but declining revenue. Different year-ends limit any like-for-like conclusion. [1–4]

**Evidence gaps and uncertainty:** Tesla’s precise figures in the table come from a secondary analysis; its 2025 filing corroborated rounded revenue and attributable net income, but the research did not independently verify every exact figure from the filing. Net margins use income attributable to owners over consolidated revenue, excluding noncontrolling interests from the numerator. Walmart’s FY2025 net income is sourced from the comparative figures in its subsequent annual report. [1,2,4]
