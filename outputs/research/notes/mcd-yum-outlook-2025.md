## Research Question
How do McDonald’s and Yum! Brands’ outlooks compare, including guidance, initiatives and growth opportunities, using FY2025 results and newer available developments?

## Key Findings
- [Fact] McDonald’s expects approximately 2,600 global restaurant openings in 2026 and $3.7–$3.9 billion in capital expenditures, mostly for new units in the U.S. and International Operated Markets. These are expectations, not completed openings or spending. [Investor overview](https://corporate.mcdonalds.com/content/dam/sites/corp/nfl/pdf/Investor%20Overview%20Deck_8.14.26.pdf)
- [Fact] Yum’s FY2025 results identify two growth platforms: Taco Bell’s full-year same-store sales rose 7%, and KFC built nearly 3,000 stores. These are outcomes, not forecasts. [FY2025 release](https://investors.yum.com/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/Yum-4Q25-Earnings-Release.pdf?_sp=11fdb86e-5959-482a-a7c6-f0902e0f2759)
- [Fact] Yum agreed to sell Pizza Hut for $2.7 billion and initially expected about $2.3 billion in net proceeds, excluding the earn-out; its later Q3 2026 release reports a roughly $1.7 billion pre-tax gain related to the sales. The expected proceeds are not verified actual proceeds, and the gain is not cash proceeds. [Sale announcement](https://investors.yum.com/news-events/financial-releases/news-details/2026/Yum-Brands-Inc--Enters-into-Agreements-to-Sell-Pizza-Hut-for-2-7-Billion/default.aspx); [Q3 release](https://investors.yum.com/files/content_files/YUM-3Q26-Earnings-Release.pdf).
- [Interpretation] McDonald’s quantified outlook emphasizes funded physical expansion. Yum’s growth opportunity is increasingly centered on its remaining brands after the Pizza Hut sales; execution and the eventual use of sale proceeds matter. The available evidence does not support a numerical earnings-growth comparison.

## Financial Facts
- **McDonald’s:** 2026 guidance: $3.7–$3.9 billion capex; approximately 2,600 gross openings, including about 750 in the U.S. and International Operated Markets and more than 1,800 by developmental licensees and affiliates. Company investor overview: https://corporate.mcdonalds.com/content/dam/sites/corp/nfl/pdf/Investor%20Overview%20Deck_8.14.26.pdf
- **Yum:** FY2025 Taco Bell same-store sales growth 7%; KFC nearly 3,000 new builds. Company release, February 4, 2026: https://investors.yum.com/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/Yum-4Q25-Earnings-Release.pdf?_sp=11fdb86e-5959-482a-a7c6-f0902e0f2759
- **Yum:** Q3 2026 reported an approximately $1.7 billion pre-tax gain related to Pizza Hut sales. Company release: https://investors.yum.com/files/content_files/YUM-3Q26-Earnings-Release.pdf

## Caveats
