## Research Question
What was Microsoft’s fiscal 2026 annual revenue, and had the full year been officially reported as of November 7, 2026?

## Key Findings
- [Fact] Fiscal 2026 ended June 30, 2026. Microsoft officially reported full-year revenue of **$331.8 billion** on July 29, 2026, up 18% year over year.
- [Fact] Microsoft’s fiscal 2026 ran from July 1, 2025 through June 30, 2026; it is not calendar year 2026.

## Financial Facts
- FY2026 revenue: **$331.8 billion**, up 18% from FY2025; full-year result, not year-to-date (Microsoft investor-relations earnings release, July 29, 2026: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/earnings/fy-2026-q4/press-release-webcast).
- Microsoft’s 2026 annual report also reports FY2026 revenue of $331.8 billion (company annual report: https://www.microsoft.com/investor/reports/ar26/index.html).

## Sources
- Microsoft Investor Relations, FY2026 Q4 earnings release, July 29, 2026: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/earnings/fy-2026-q4/press-release-webcast
- Microsoft, 2026 Annual Report: https://www.microsoft.com/investor/reports/ar26/index.html

## Caveats
The reported amount is rounded to the nearest $0.1 billion. Fiscal 2026 does not represent the January–December 2026 calendar year; no calendar-year revenue is inferred.
