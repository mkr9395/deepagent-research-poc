## Research Question
What principal risks do Apple and Tesla disclose in their latest available fiscal-year SEC filings, and what material recent enforcement developments can be verified?

## Key Findings
- [Fact] **Apple — operational and cyber:** Its FY2026 10-K identifies dependence on complex IT systems across Apple and its global supply chain. Disruptions can arise from power or telecommunications failures, war, ransomware and other cybersecurity incidents. [Apple 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000026/aapl-20260926.htm)
- [Fact] **Tesla — market and technology:** Its FY2025 10-K states that future growth and success depend on demand for its electric vehicles and adoption of autonomous-driving solutions. [Tesla 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm)
- [Fact] **Tesla — company-specific capital allocation:** The filing warns that technologies and initiatives associated with the 2025 CEO Performance Award, including product goals, could be misaligned with consumer demand, potentially diverting investment from other opportunities. [Tesla 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm)
- [Interpretation] The retrieved disclosures illustrate different exposures: Apple's cited risk concerns operational continuity across a global supply chain, while Tesla's explicitly ties growth to EV demand and autonomous-driving adoption. This is not a complete ranking of either company's risks.

## Financial Facts
- Apple FY2026, ended September 26, 2026: the 10-K identifies credit risk on trade accounts receivable; no exposure amount was retrieved. The 10-K was filed November 3, 2026. [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000026/aapl-20260926.htm); [Apple filing index](https://investor.apple.com/sec-filings/default.aspx).
- Tesla FY2025, ended December 31, 2025: the CEO-award disclosure identifies a risk of forgone financial returns from alternative investments; no quantified loss was retrieved. [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm).
