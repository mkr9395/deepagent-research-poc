## Research Question
Which substantive principal risks did NVIDIA and Tesla disclose in their respective fiscal 2025 10-Ks, and what company-specific evidence supports comparison?

## Key Findings
- [Fact] Both historical 10-Ks were located, but the retrieved extracts did **not** expose substantive Item 1A risk text. Tesla’s filing index places Item 1A on pages 12–27. The specific risks in those sections therefore remain unverified from the retrieved evidence. [NVIDIA](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581025000023/nvda-20250126.htm); [Tesla](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm).
- [Fact] Tesla’s fiscal-2025 Q4 update provides alternative company evidence of risks concerning factory ramps, procuring battery cells—including through its own manufacturing—and international operations and expansion. Its cautionary language references the 10-K filed January 30, 2025 and subsequent 10-Qs; these points are **not verified as Item 1A disclosures in Tesla’s December 2025 10-K**. [Tesla Q4 update](https://assets-ir.tesla.com/tesla-contents/IR/TSLA-Q4-2025-Update.pdf).
- [Fact] Tesla’s FY2025 10-K says it entered autonomous ride-hailing with a Robotaxi launch in June 2025. This establishes company-specific operating context, but the retrieved passage does not identify a corresponding Item 1A risk. [Tesla 10-K](https://ir.tesla.com/_flysystem/s3/sec/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231-gen.pdf).
- [Interpretation] A substantive like-for-like risk comparison is not supportable from these extracts: NVIDIA-specific FY2025 risk details and Tesla’s December 2025 Item 1A wording remain unverified.

## Financial Facts
- No financial performance figures are included: the brief concerns risk disclosures only.

## Caveats
NVIDIA’s fiscal 2025 ended January 26, 2025; Tesla’s ended December 31, 2025, so their disclosure dates and exposure windows differ materially. The primary filings were located, but their substantive risk-factor passages were not retrieved in the available search results. Tesla’s Q4 update is an authoritative contemporaneous alternative, not proof of the contents of the later-filed 2025 10-K. No conclusion about the relative importance or completeness of either company’s risks is warranted.
