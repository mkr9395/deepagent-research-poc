## Research Question
Compare Apple and Tesla’s major financial and regulatory risks using their latest available fiscal-year filings and significant recent regulatory actions.

## Key Findings
- [Fact] Apple’s FY2025 10-K warns that adverse legal proceedings or government investigations could harm its business, results and financial condition. Its App Store rules are a concrete regulatory exposure. ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/aapl-20250927.htm))
- [Fact] On April 23, 2025, the European Commission fined Apple €500 million, finding its app-steering practices noncompliant with the Digital Markets Act. Apple appealed, according to subsequent secondary reporting; an appeal is not a reversal. ([Goodwin](https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2025/06/insights-practices-antc-10-key-takeaways-from-the-european); [EU Insider](https://www.euinsider.eu/news/apple-appeals-eu500-million-eu-fine-for-digital-markets-act-violation))
- [Fact] Tesla’s FY2025 filing identifies automotive regulatory-credit revenue as exposed to changes in regulation, production, sales and demand from other automakers. It says 2025 governmental actions, including the OBBBA, restricted certain programs tied to its products. ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm))
- [Fact] Secondary reporting describes a U.S. NHTSA preliminary investigation opened October 7, 2025 into Tesla Full Self-Driving’s response to road signals, signs and markings. An investigation is not a finding of a defect or a recall. ([Aguiar Injury Lawyers](https://aguiarinjurylawyers.com/tesla-fsd-investigation-2026))
- [Interpretation] Apple’s cited exposure centers on app-distribution rules and enforcement; Tesla’s includes both regulation-sensitive credit revenue and vehicle-safety scrutiny. Tesla’s automotive sales fell approximately 9.2% year over year, calculated from its filing, adding financial pressure. ([Tesla SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm))

## Financial Facts
- **Apple:** FY ended September 27, 2025; a comparable balance-sheet or cash-flow risk figure was not found in the retrieved filing excerpts. ([SEC, company filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/aapl-20250927.htm))
- **Tesla:** FY ended December 31, 2025 automotive sales were $65.821 billion versus $72.480 billion in FY2024; automotive regulatory-credit revenue decreased $770 million, or 28%, year over year. ([SEC, company filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm))
- **Apple regulatory penalty:** €500 million, announced April 23, 2025; appealed. ([Goodwin](https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2025/06/insights-practices-antc-10-key-takeaways-from-the-european); [EU Insider](https://www.euinsider.eu/news/apple-appeals-eu500-million-eu-fine-for-digital-markets-act-violation))
