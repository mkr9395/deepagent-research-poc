## Research Question
How did McDonald’s and Yum! Brands compare on revenue growth and profitability in their latest available full fiscal years?

## Key Findings
- [Interpretation] Yum’s FY2025 revenue grew **8.8%** versus McDonald’s **3.7%**, calculated from reported revenue. McDonald’s reported 4% growth, or 2% in constant currency; Yum reported 9% growth.
- [Interpretation] McDonald’s had higher calculated operating and net margins in both years: **46.1% and 31.9%** in 2025, versus Yum’s **31.3% and 19.0%**. McDonald’s operating margin expanded about 0.9 percentage points; Yum’s contracted about 0.5 points.
- [Fact] Both companies receive franchised-restaurant revenues as well as company-operated restaurant sales. Yum separately reports franchise advertising and other service contributions. These different revenue streams affect the denominator of reported margins.
- [Interpretation] Corporate revenue and margins are not directly comparable measures of restaurant-level sales or economics: franchise revenue and company-operated sales reflect different business models. Yum’s mix is disclosed below; a comparable full-year McDonald’s revenue breakdown was not extracted.

## Financial Facts
Both fiscal years ended December 31. Dollar figures are USD millions; figures are GAAP. Growth and margins below are calculated from reported, rounded figures; operating margin = operating income/revenue, net margin = net income/revenue.

- **McDonald’s:** 2025 / 2024 revenue **$26,885 / $25,920**; operating income **$12,393 / $11,712**; net income **$8,563 / $8,223**. Calculated 2025 revenue growth: **3.7%**. Operating margins: **46.1% / 45.2%**; net margins: **31.9% / 31.7%**. Earnings release, February 11, 2026: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/63908/000006390826000032/exhibit991-12312025.htm
- **Yum! Brands:** 2025 / 2024 revenue **$8,214 / $7,549**; operating profit **$2,574 / $2,403**; net income **$1,559 / $1,486**. Calculated 2025 revenue growth: **8.8%**. Operating margins: **31.3% / 31.8%**; net margins: **19.0% / 19.7%**. Company annual report: https://s2.q4cdn.com/890585342/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/annual-report-2025/index.html
- **Yum revenue mix:** 2025 / 2024 company sales **$2,945 / $2,552**; franchise and property revenues **$3,473 / $3,295**; franchise contributions for advertising and other services **$1,796 / $1,702**. Company annual report: https://s2.q4cdn.com/890585342/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/annual-report-2025/index.html

## Caveats
Yum’s release refers to the prior-year 53rd-week lap when discussing adjusted operating profit; reported year-over-year growth is not a pure equal-week comparison. Currency movements also affect reported growth. Margins use GAAP corporate revenue, not systemwide restaurant sales; reported operating income includes special items. McDonald’s comparable full-year franchise/company revenue mix was not extracted.
