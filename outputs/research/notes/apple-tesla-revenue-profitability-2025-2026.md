## Research Question
Compare Apple and Tesla’s latest full fiscal-year revenue and profitability, with recent quarterly evidence.

## Key Findings
- [Interpretation] Apple’s FY2025 revenue rose approximately 6.4% year over year; Tesla’s fell approximately 2.9%, calculated from the figures below. Apple’s revenue was roughly 4.4 times Tesla’s.
- [Interpretation] Apple’s calculated FY2025 net margin was approximately 26.9%, versus approximately 4.0% for Tesla on a net-income-attributable-to-common-stockholders basis. The latter differs slightly from total consolidated net income.
- [Fact] In the recent quarters found, Apple’s net income increased year over year, while Tesla reported quarterly revenue growth. These are different measures and quarters.

## Financial Facts
All amounts are US dollars. Growth and margins are calculated unless described as reported.

- **Apple FY2025**, ended September 27, 2025: revenue $416.16 billion, versus $391.04 billion in FY2024; revenue growth approximately 6.4% (Yahoo Finance, secondary: https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/AAPL/financials). Net income $112.010 billion, versus $93.736 billion in FY2024 (company 10-K: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/aapl-20250927.htm). Calculated net margin: approximately 26.9%, using the secondary-source revenue.
- **Tesla FY2025**, ended December 31, 2025: revenue $94.83 billion, down $2.86 billion from FY2024; implied FY2024 revenue approximately $97.69 billion and growth approximately −2.9%. Net income attributable to common stockholders was $3.79 billion; calculated attributable net margin approximately 4.0% (company 10-K: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm).
- **Apple Q2 FY2026**, ended March 28, 2026: net income $29.578 billion versus $24.780 billion a year earlier, up approximately 19.4% (calculated; company 10-Q: https://s2.q4cdn.com/470004039/files/doc_earnings/2026/q2/filing/10Q-Q2-2026-as-filed.pdf).
- **Tesla Q2 FY2026**: revenue $28.2 billion, up a reported 26% year over year (company quarterly update: https://assets-ir.tesla.com/tesla-contents/IR/TSLA-Q2-2026-Update.pdf).

## Caveats
Apple’s year ends in September and Tesla’s in December. Apple’s annual revenue figures are secondary-source figures because the primary-source revenue values were not returned in the search results; the annual growth and margin use rounded revenue figures. Tesla’s margin uses income attributable to common stockholders, not total net income. Apple’s Q2 revenue and Tesla’s Q2 net income were not found in the retrieved excerpts; quarterly profitability comparisons are therefore incomplete. The quarters shown are recent, not necessarily the latest available.
