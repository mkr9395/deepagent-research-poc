## Research Question
How did Apple and Tesla compare on revenue, year-over-year growth, net income, and operating and net margins in their latest available full fiscal years as of November 8, 2026?

## Key Findings
- [Fact] Apple’s FY2026 revenue was $478.834 billion, versus Tesla’s FY2025 revenue of approximately $94.83 billion; their fiscal years ended almost nine months apart.
- [Interpretation] Calculated revenue growth was approximately 15.1% for Apple versus a 2.9% decline for Tesla, each compared with its own preceding fiscal year.
- [Interpretation] Calculated net margins were approximately 27.6% for Apple and 4.1% for Tesla. Apple’s calculated operating margin was 33.0%; Tesla’s operating income was not verified in the retrieved filing excerpt, so its operating margin cannot be compared reliably here.

## Financial Facts
- Apple, FY ended September 26, 2026: revenue $478.834 billion; FY2025 revenue $416.161 billion; operating income $158.063 billion (company 10-K: https://s2.q4cdn.com/470004039/files/doc_earnings/2026/q4/filing/10K-Q4-2026-as-filed.pdf). FY2026 net income $132.175 billion (secondary reproduction of 10-K: https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/AAPL/10-k-apple-inc-files-annual-report-e8799a9bf72c.html).
- Tesla, FY ended December 31, 2025: revenue approximately $94.83 billion, down approximately $2.86 billion from FY2024; consolidated net income $3.855 billion; net income attributable to common stockholders approximately $3.79 billion (company 10-K: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm).

## Caveats
Apple’s net-income figure was retrieved from a secondary reproduction of its filing, not from the primary filing excerpt. Tesla’s operating income and operating margin were not found in the retrieved primary excerpt. Tesla revenue and its prior-year change are rounded, so its calculated growth and net margin are approximate. Net margins use consolidated net income, before any deduction for noncontrolling interests, divided by revenue; operating margin is operating income divided by revenue. Apple’s September-ending FY2026 and Tesla’s calendar FY2025 cover different periods; Tesla FY2026 was not yet complete on the research date.
