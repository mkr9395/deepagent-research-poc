## Research Question
How do Apple and Tesla compare on latest reported fiscal-year revenue growth and profitability, and on subsequently reported results?

## Key Findings
- [Fact] Apple’s FY2026 revenue was $478.834bn, up 15.1% year over year (calculated); Tesla’s FY2025 revenue was $94.827bn, down 2.9% (calculated).
- [Interpretation] Apple’s FY2026 net margin was 27.6%, versus Tesla’s 4.1% in FY2025, calculated from consolidated net income and revenue. Their fiscal periods differ.
- [Fact] Tesla subsequently reported Q3 2026 revenue of $28.05bn, down approximately 0.2% year over year, and consolidated net income of $804m. Its calculated net margin was approximately 2.9%.
- [Fact] Apple’s FY2026 results include its quarter ended September 26, 2026; separate fourth-quarter financial figures were not retrieved.

## Financial Facts
- **Apple, FY ended September 26, 2026:** Revenue $478.834bn versus $416.161bn in FY2025; gross profit $233.602bn; operating income $158.063bn; net income $132.175bn. Calculated gross, operating and net margins: 48.8%, 33.0% and 27.6%, respectively. Company 10-K: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000026/aapl-20260926.htm
- **Tesla, FY ended December 31, 2025:** Revenue $94.827bn versus $97.690bn in FY2024, calculated by summing the filing’s two segment revenues; consolidated net income $3.855bn. Calculated net margin: 4.1%. Company 10-K: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm
- **Tesla, Q3 ended September 30, 2026:** Revenue $28.05bn, down $49m year over year; net income attributable to common stockholders $787m, down from $1.373bn. Company 10-Q: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026066775/tsla-20260930.htm. Consolidated net income was $804m versus $1.389bn; October 21, 2026 company results: https://ir.tesla.com/_flysystem/s3/sec/000162828026066738/tsla-20261021-gen.pdf

## Caveats
Apple’s fiscal year ended in September 2026, Tesla’s in December 2025; the annual results are not contemporaneous. Tesla’s Q3 consolidated net income includes noncontrolling interests, whereas the common-stockholder figure excludes them. Tesla Q3 revenue is rounded in the filing excerpt, making the calculated growth and net margin approximate. Apple’s separate Q4 figures and Tesla’s annual gross and operating margins were not retrieved. No later Apple quarter was identified as reported by November 7, 2026.
