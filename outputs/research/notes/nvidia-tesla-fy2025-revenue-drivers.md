## Research Question
What were NVIDIA’s and Tesla’s fiscal 2025 revenues, year-over-year growth, principal revenue drivers and fiscal year-end dates?

## Key Findings
- [Fact] NVIDIA’s fiscal year ended **January 26, 2025**; revenue was **$130.497 billion**, up **114%** year over year. Its annual report attributes Data Center growth to demand for Hopper accelerated computing for large language models, recommendation engines and generative AI. Data Center revenue grew 142% year over year. [NVIDIA annual report](https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/doc_financials/2025/annual/NVIDIA-2025-Annual-Report.pdf); [secondary financial-statement compilation](https://sec-api.io/media/prompts/nvidia-financial-statements/report.pdf).
- [Fact] Tesla’s fiscal year ended **December 31, 2025**; revenue was **$94.827 billion**, down **2.93% calculated** from 2024. Automotive revenue was its largest revenue line, but fell; increased energy and services revenue partly offset the decline. Tesla attributed energy growth primarily to increased Megapack and Powerwall deployments, partly offset by lower Megapack average selling prices. [Tesla 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm).
- [Interpretation] NVIDIA’s revenue exceeded Tesla’s by **$35.670 billion**, calculated, but the fiscal years ended more than 11 months apart and cover different 12-month periods.

## Financial Facts
- **NVIDIA:** FY2025 total revenue **$130.497 billion**, versus FY2024 **$60.922 billion**; Data Center FY2025 **$115.186 billion**, versus **$47.525 billion**. Exact amounts from a [secondary financial-statement compilation](https://sec-api.io/media/prompts/nvidia-financial-statements/report.pdf); the [company annual report](https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/doc_financials/2025/annual/NVIDIA-2025-Annual-Report.pdf) reports rounded total revenue of $130.5 billion and 114% growth.
- **Tesla:** FY2025 total revenue **$94.827 billion**, versus FY2024 **$97.690 billion**. FY2025 automotive revenue was **$69.526 billion**, versus **$77.070 billion**; energy generation and storage **$12.771 billion**, versus **$10.086 billion**; services and other **$12.530 billion**, versus **$10.534 billion**. [Company 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm).

## Caveats
Fiscal year-ends differ substantially; these are each company’s FY2025, not aligned calendar periods or their latest reported years. NVIDIA’s exact revenue and Data Center figures are from a secondary compilation: the retrieved company report excerpt confirmed only rounded total revenue and growth. Tesla’s percentage decline and the revenue difference are calculated from the cited figures. Tesla’s automotive revenue line excludes services and other, unlike its broader automotive segment total.
