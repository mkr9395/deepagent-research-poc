## Research Question
What material risks did NVIDIA and Tesla disclose in their fiscal 2025 annual filings, and how did their exposures differ?

## Key Findings
- [Fact] NVIDIA's FY2025 ended January 26, 2025; Tesla's FY2025 ended December 31, 2025. Their filings therefore cover different periods. [NVIDIA](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm); [Tesla](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm).
- [Fact] NVIDIA disclosed three direct customers individually accounting for 12%, 11% and 11% of FY2025 sales; these were primarily attributable to Compute & Networking. [NVIDIA FY2025 10-K](https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/177440d5-3b32-4185-8cc8-95500a9dc783.pdf).
- [Interpretation] The three customer shares demonstrate concentrated direct-customer exposure: together they accounted for 34% of sales, calculated. This is an inference from the disclosure, not a retrieved quotation from the risk factors. The direct customers need not be the ultimate end customers.
- [Fact] Tesla's 2025 10-K identifies an Item 1A risk-factors section, but the available search extracts did not provide its substantive risk disclosures. A defensible comparison of the companies' principal disclosed risks could not be completed. [Tesla FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm).

## Financial Facts
- NVIDIA FY2025 direct-customer shares: 12%, 11% and 11% of total sales; 34% combined (calculated). Company 10-K: https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/177440d5-3b32-4185-8cc8-95500a9dc783.pdf
- Tesla FY2025: no comparable quantified risk exposure was retrieved from its annual filing.

## Caveats
The search extracts did not retrieve substantive Item 1A risk-factor text for either company. Consequently, the principal risk lists and Tesla's documented exposures remain unverified here; customer concentration alone is not a complete NVIDIA risk assessment. The fiscal year-ends differ by approximately 11 months. These are historical FY2025 filings, not necessarily the latest disclosures; NVIDIA's FY2026 10-K was also located, but the question specifies FY2025.
