## Research Question
What are McDonald’s and Yum! Brands’ material financial, regulatory, market and business risks, based on their latest available annual disclosures and relevant developments? Both FY2025 years ended December 31, 2025.

## Key Findings
- [Fact] **McDonald’s — potential business and cyber risk:** Its FY2025 10-K identifies franchisees, vendors, suppliers and service providers as sources of cyber risk. A third-party incident could compromise company data or disrupt business despite mitigation measures. This is a disclosed possibility, not an identified incident. [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/63908/000006390826000035/mcd-20251231.htm)
- [Fact] **Yum — potential cyber risk:** Its FY2025 10-K identifies ransomware, malicious software, credential stuffing, denial-of-service attacks and other threats, including from nation-state actors. These are potential threats; the disclosure does not establish that these particular attacks occurred in 2025. [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1041061/000104106126000084/yum-20251231.htm)
- [Fact] **Yum — actual financial exposure:** It recorded $27 million in net bad-debt expense in FY2025. The excerpt does not establish the amount attributable specifically to franchisees. [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1041061/000104106126000084/yum-20251231.htm)
- [Fact] **Yum — actual geopolitical impact and continuing potential exposure:** In its earlier FY2024 10-K, received February 19, 2025, Yum said Middle East and Russia–Ukraine conflicts had adversely affected it and could continue to do so. This is historical disclosure, not confirmation of a specific FY2025 or later impact. [Filing reproduction](https://content.edgar-online.com/ExternalLink/EDGAR/0001041061-25-000013.html?hash=52abde08dcd64dd2fe5e817e3a3e821c19713e490811d4b74c2e1d40d2d27333&dest=yum-20241231_htm)

## Financial Facts
- Yum FY2025 net bad-debt expense: **$27 million**, versus $28 million in FY2024 (company 10-K: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1041061/000104106126000084/yum-20251231.htm).
- A comparable McDonald’s credit-risk figure was not found in the available excerpts.

## Caveats
The retrieved FY2025 excerpts do not substantiate a complete ranking of material risks or adequately cover each company’s regulatory/legal, market and financial risks. Specific recent developments beyond the historical geopolitical disclosure were not verified. The geopolitical evidence is from a third-party filing reproduction and predates the FY2025 filing; current impacts are unverified. Absence of a risk above does not mean it is immaterial.
