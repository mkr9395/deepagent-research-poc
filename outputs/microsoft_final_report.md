# Microsoft Annual Revenue in 2026

## Executive Summary
Microsoft reported **$331.8 billion in revenue for fiscal 2026**, which ended June 30, 2026. [1]

## Overall Comparison and Evidence Gaps
The figure covers July 1, 2025, through June 30, 2026, rather than calendar year 2026, and is rounded to the nearest $0.1 billion. [1] Calendar-year revenue was not established in the research.
